One family at a time

Every pattern in the generator comes from one of six small functions, called families. Each family is handed a rectangle, a spacing and a random number generator, and returns shapes. Here you can watch a single family react to its knobs. Spacing is the distance between motif centres, in page units where 100 units is one inch.

All families at the same spacing

The same spacing looks very different from family to family: a spot is one closed shape per cell, while chevrons put several lines in the same distance. That is why the generator scales each family's spacing with the DENSITY table in generator.js. The captions show the spacing after that scaling, which is what the generator would actually draw. Slide to compare.

Anatomy of an animal

An animal is a jigsaw of closed pieces, drawn back to front. Each piece is shaded a different gray here so you can see where one ends and the next begins; the arrow in a piece is its axis, the direction the wavy band dividers cross. None of this shading is ever printed.
